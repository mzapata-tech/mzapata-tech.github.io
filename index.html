// origin_story

How MediVeins was born.

Every good project starts with a real problem. This one started with my girlfriend getting a new job.

the problem

A clinic with no system

My girlfriend was hired as a secretary at Clínica Integral Mediveins — a real medical clinic with 10 doctors. On her first week she realized there was no CRM, no digital calendar, no patient database. Everything was manual, scattered, and time-consuming.

the constraint

"It has to be free."

The clinic is small. Budget was zero. So expensive software was out of the question. She asked me if I could build something that connected Google Calendar, WhatsApp Business, and some kind of patient database — for free. I said yes.

the solution

Google Sheets as the backbone

I chose Google Sheets as the database — free, familiar to the secretary, and already inside Google's ecosystem. I built a custom sidebar UI directly inside Sheets using Apps Script, so she could manage 10 doctors' appointments without learning any new software.

the reality

Bugs. Lots of them.

The first version was rough. WhatsApp messages showed wrong times, Calendar sync broke, patient names were duplicating. But because I was using AI to help me build, I could find and fix errors faster than ever. Each bug became a lesson.

the evolution

Then I added an AI agent

Once the foundation was stable, I asked: what if AI didn't just help me build the system — what if it ran part of it? I built Maureen, a WhatsApp AI agent powered by Claude. She handles patient conversations, checks real-time availability, books appointments, and sends confirmations. The secretary just monitors.
